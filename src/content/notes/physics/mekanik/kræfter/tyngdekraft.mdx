# Tyngdekraft

\begin{equation}
F = G \frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}
\label{eq:Kompliceret}
\end{equation}

Konstant $G\approx 6.6743\cdot10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m}^{3}\mathrm{kg}^{-1}\mathrm{s}^{-2}$

\begin{equation}
F = mg
\label{eq:Simpel}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
g = G\frac{m_2}{r^2}
\label{eq:Tyngdeacceleration}
\end{equation}

\begin{calculator}
g = [G]\frac{[m_2]}{[r]^2}
\end{calculator}

Radius ved polerne: $r_\text{pol}\approx 6\ 356.8\ \mathrm{km}$
Radius ved ækvator: $r_\text{ækv}\approx 6\ 378.1\ \mathrm{km}$
Masse: $m\approx 5.972\cdot10^{24}\ \mathrm{kg}$
